# diogenes / lab

**We build models that see, imagine, and play virtual worlds — and we do it in the open.**

Four models, one loop — see · imagine · predict · act.

Diogenes of Sinope walked through the market at noon carrying a lit lamp, looking for one honest
man. We took the name because the job has the same shape: **go and look at what is actually there**.

## The stack

Not four products — **four faculties of a single agent**, wired into a loop. Each is a research
project at its own stage of maturity, and each one makes the next possible.

| | Model | Faculty | | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Visual** | Perceive — the eyes | Reads the screen in real time, on-device | `Alpha` |
| 02 | **World** | Imagine — the imagination | An interactive world learned from gameplay video | `Research` |
| 03 | **Action** | Predict — causal intuition | Predicts how an action changes the world | `Research` |
| 04 | **Player** | Act — the hands | Plays the game, and wins | `Alpha` |

**See** what is on the screen. **Imagine** the world it implies. **Predict** what an action would do
to that world. **Act** — then look again at what actually happened.

→ Where each one really stands, including what is blocked:
**[ROADMAP](https://github.com/DiogenesModel/research/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)**

## Why games

Games are the most signal-dense, consequence-verifiable and compliantly collectable source of
embodied data we know of. Every frame has a ground truth about what happened next, the environment
resets for free, and — unlike the physical world — nobody gets hurt when the policy is bad.

Games are the starting point, not the destination. The loop an agent learns here is the loop a robot
needs.

## How we work

1. **Open science** — publishing the reasoning matters more than publishing the result.
2. **Verified by refutation** — load-bearing numbers face independent verifiers whose job is to
   *refute* them. Two of three refutations and the claim is dropped, not softened. This is the rule
   we most often lose arguments to.
3. **Efficient and self-sufficient** — small models, local first. Every encoder starts from
   published open weights; we never pre-train one from scratch.
4. **Compliant by design** — first-party collection behind a consent gate; never third-party
   scraping. If the only way to get a dataset is to take it, we do without the dataset.
5. **Embodied ambition** — games are where we prove it; general embodied intelligence is where it
   points.
